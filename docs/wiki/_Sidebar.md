### Level 1 · Deploy
The four Level 1 chapters. All build on **L1.1**; L1.4 needs L1.3. **L1.3 does not need L1.2.** Costs are **running totals** with every chapter still deployed, billed while deployed.

| | Chapter | Running cost |
|---|---|---|
|🟢| [L1.1 — Core Deployment](Curriculum-L1-1-Core-Deployment) | ~$0.24/hr |
|🟡| [L1.2 — Architecture Expansion](Curriculum-L1-2-Architecture-Expansion) | ~$1.65/hr |
|🟠| [L1.3 — Multi-Service Application](Curriculum-L1-3-Multi-Service-Application) | ~$1.73/hr |
|🔴| [L1.4 — Production-Ready Platform](Curriculum-L1-4-Production-Platform) | ~$1.84/hr |
